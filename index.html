Features

Everything You Need on Stage

Built by a working musician, for working musicians. ShowTime handles the tech so you can focus on the music.

Master Device Command & Control

The master device is the conductor. Change songs, start the backing track, trigger the slideshow or visualiser — everything fires simultaneously across every connected device. Each musician turns their own pages independently with realistic page-curl animations, foot pedal, or swipe. The band just follows. No lag, no confusion.

Instant Transpose

Transpose any chart up or down with one tap — the new key persists per song. Or set an instrument mode across the whole setlist, so Eb, Bb, F and G players always read their own key automatically.

Eb · Bb · F · G setlist transpose

Per-Song Video & Visual Mood

Link videos or still images to any song — with cinematic transitions, Ken Burns zoom, and fade effects. Or let the built-in visualiser set the mood, pulsing and throbbing to the beat of the music in real time. This area is expanding significantly in the coming years.

Open Mic & Backing Band

Run open mic nights with ease. Manage guest performers, track the running order, and provide backing charts to walk-up artists — all from the same system your band already uses.

Foot Pedal & Keyboard Navigation

Just four buttons — up, down, left, right — that adapt to whatever's on screen.

In a list like your playlists or search results, up and down move through it, right opens the selected song or playlist, and left steps back out.

On the menu bar, left and right move across the toolbar buttons while up or down run the one you've landed on — so up and down turn the page by default (next and previous), and on the transpose control they shift the key up or down.

And Home is smart: tap down to jump straight back to where you were in the list, or up to start a fresh search — the box clears and the on-screen keyboard appears, ready to type.

Works with any Bluetooth foot pedal or keyboard, so your hands stay on the instrument and your eyes stay on the stage.

Annotations

Add personal notes, cues, and reminders right where they're needed — pinned to specific parts of a song, with instrument-specific colour coding. Go further and drop in music notation, from a few bars to nail an exact riff all the way to full-page sheet music. And like everything in ShowTime, the notation transposes with you — notes and key signature shift to match. Drag any annotation anywhere and it stays put.

Instant Band Onboarding

Musicians join with a single QR code scan — no app install, no account. Every chart is instantly available on their device, in their key, synced to the master in real time.

Crew Messaging

Send colour-coded alerts to your sound engineer, lighting tech, or stage manager mid-show. They acknowledge with one tap — you see it confirmed instantly. No shouting across the stage.

Multiple Chart Formats

HTML chord charts, PDF fakebook pages, and MusicXML sheet music all in one song. Switch between arrangements in one tap — jazz chart, simplified chart, or the full score.

Rehearsal & Practice Links

Attach useful links to any song — YouTube tutorials, Spotify reference tracks, chord diagrams, backing track sites. Band members tap through to practice material straight from the chart screen, before and after the gig.

Backing Track to the PA

Attach audio files directly to a song. When the show starts, ShowTime automatically routes the backing track to whichever device is nearest the mixing desk — no cables, no fuss. The sound engineer confirms their device as the audio output with one tap.

Circle of Fifths

Interactive Circle of Fifths popup for quick key reference, chord relationships, and transposition guidance — right there on your performance screen when you need it.

How It Works

Up and Running in Minutes

1

Import Your Charts

Paste from Word, import RTF files, link PDF fakebook pages, or type directly. Your existing chord charts work perfectly — 3,000+ song libraries are no problem.

2

Build Your Setlists

Organise songs into playlists for each gig. Drag to reorder on the night. Add pre-show images and intermission slides to each playlist.

Setup

Two Ways to Run ShowTime

Go fully offline with your own gear, or let the cloud handle your library — your choice, and the two stay in sync both ways.

⇅ Run it either way — your library stays in sync.
Edit offline and it syncs up next time you're online; edit in the cloud and it syncs down. Reciprocal — both directions.

At the gig, a Cloud account still runs on your local Wi-Fi like Standalone for the band's screens — the cloud just holds your library & billing.

ShowTime runs a local web server on your main device (the Master — typically a laptop or Raspberry Pi). Other band members open a browser on their phone or tablet and type in your device's IP address. When you change songs, transpose, or turn the page, every connected screen updates instantly over your local WiFi network. No internet required, no accounts, no app installs.

The Master device (where you run ShowTime) can be any Windows, Mac, or Linux machine — or a Raspberry Pi 5 for a compact, silent stage solution. Band members just need any device with a browser: phone, tablet, or laptop. For projection, connect any HDMI display or TV. A small WiFi router dedicated to the band is recommended for reliable sync at a venue.

The paid Windows standalone app uses a local file-based database (SQLite) stored on your own machine. You own it completely. Point it at a folder inside your OneDrive, Google Drive, or Dropbox and your songs automatically back up and sync across your devices — no subscription to us required for storage.

Why file-based? Musicians know how to copy files. Your entire song library is a single file — copy it to a USB stick, drag it to a new laptop, or let your cloud drive handle it automatically. No "backup and restore" wizards, no export/import headaches. Just files.

This makes the standalone app ideal for no-internet gig situations — everything runs locally, your data never leaves your machine.
